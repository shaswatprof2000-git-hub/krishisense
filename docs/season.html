How it works

Each hour the model removes the crop's water use, ETc = Kc × ET0, from the root zone. ET0 is estimated with the Hargreaves method from simulated temperatures. Rain and irrigation add water back. At 07:00 KrishiSense compares the root-zone depletion Dr with the crop's readily available water RAW = p × TAW (FAO Irrigation Paper 56):

  • Irrigate now if Dr ≥ RAW: refill to field capacity. Pump minutes = (Dr − expected rain) ÷ efficiency × area ÷ pump flow.
  • Skip, rain due if likely rain (amount × probability × 0.8) covers ≥ 60% of the need.
  • Irrigate today if today's crop water use will cross the line before tomorrow morning.

The same logic runs in the real KrishiSense server. This page is a simplified, illustrative model with simulated weather. Real savings depend on the field, calibration and the farmer. Source code: github.com/shaswatprof2000-git-hub/krishisense
